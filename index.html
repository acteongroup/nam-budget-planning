Scenario lab

How scenarios work

The basic loop

Pick a tool, click Preview to see exactly what would change, then Apply to plan. Nothing touches the plan until you apply. Every apply saves a snapshot first, so Undo last scenario (or Snapshots → Restore) always gets you back.

Shift budget: spend more somewhere

  • Increase: choose an area (a group like Trade shows, or a single GL account). Use + Add another increase to raise several areas in one scenario.
  • By: a % of the area's current plan, a flat $, or $ each × qty (for example 4 KOLs at $15,000 each).
  • Add as a new line: give it a name (e.g. "4 new KOLs") to add the money as its own line item. Leave it blank to spread the increase across the area's existing lines in proportion to their size.
  • Also remove lines: open that section to drop line items in the same scenario. The money they free up counts toward paying for the increases, so less is cut elsewhere.
  • Pay for it by: Cutting other accounts keeps the total flat; Cutting only enough to stay within target only trims what pushes you over the target; Adding to the total makes no cuts.

Where cuts come from

Every other line gets a flexibility weight. Cuts are shared out by size × flexibility, so large, flexible lines give the most. No line gives more than Max cut per line.

Type of spendFlexibility
Travel & entertainmentVery high
Misc / ad hocVery high
One-time last yearHigh
Annual show / event, variable spendMedium
Subscriptions & toolsLow
Recurring commitments (retainers, contracts)Very low; none while "Protect" is ticked

In the preview, untick any line to protect it and its cut moves to the others. Take cuts from → Same cost center only keeps the offset inside one cost center.

Hit target

When a cost center or the whole plan is over its target, this suggests cuts that close exactly the gap, using the same flexibility rules. Use Never cut to fence off an area. Targets are set in the table above; type a total in its bottom row and it is split across cost centers.

Remove lines

Tick line items to drop them (or set them to $0). The preview shows the savings and how the plan compares to target. This is how to take out a vendor or sponsorship you are no longer funding. The × button on a line in the grid does the same for a single line.

Start over

Start over (top right of the Scenario lab) resets every line item to the plan as first created, from the base year and starting options you chose. Targets and snapshots are kept, and Undo brings your changes back.

Saved scenarios

Use the bar at the top of the Scenario lab: type a name, click Save scenario, and later pick it from the list and click Load. To change a saved scenario, edit the plan, pick it in the list and click Update (type a new name first to rename it too). Save the plan under a name before exploring, then Compare a snapshot to the current plan by GL account, or Restore it. The 12 newest are kept.
